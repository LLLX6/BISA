# BISA product foundation | أساس منتج بيسا

## Promise

**اكتشافاتك، قريبة منك.**
**Your discoveries, close to you.**

BISA helps people discover selected low-ticket products at nearby stores without framing them as “cheap”. The experience is energetic, premium and local.

## Launch scope

- Muscat Governorate: Muscat, Muttrah, Bawshar, Seeb, Al Amerat and Qurayyat.
- Areas remain hidden until an approved active branch exists.
- Products: OMR 0.100–2.000 each.
- Fulfillment: store pickup, merchant office delivery and merchant home delivery.
- Revenue: subscriptions, merchant advertising, supplier advertising and campaigns; initial commission is 0%.

## Shopper journey

1. Browse as a guest and choose an active location.
2. Search/list/map and compare price, store verification, stock and fulfillment.
3. Add products from one store; replacing another store’s cart requires confirmation.
4. Choose fulfillment and send the order.
5. Store confirms component availability before stock is deducted.
6. Track the order through one concise visual timeline.

## Merchant journey

1. Sign in as a shopper and submit a private merchant application.
2. Admin reviews; approval atomically activates the store, branch, role and 90-day trial.
3. Merchant opens an independent five-tab workspace: Today, Orders, Catalog, Promotions, More.
4. Add products quickly, verify stock, build proper bundles and decide orders before the deadline.
5. Supplier Hub remains merchant-only; promotions never claim results before real measurement.
