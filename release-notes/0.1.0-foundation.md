## Known limitations

- SQLite is the implemented runtime; PostgreSQL is not implemented or claimed.
- Payment, WhatsApp, Push and live map integrations are deliberately unconfigured.
- Real store catalog/data is not included; sample data is local-only behind `BISA_SEED_SAMPLE_DATA=true`.
- Production needs owner-approved hosting, persistent storage/backups, vendor selection, legal review and penetration testing.
